GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day pastry and cake delivery?

Yes! We deliver across Phnom Penh daily between 7:30 AM and 8:30 PM. For morning croissants and warm baguettes, order by 8:00 AM.

How far in advance should I order custom celebration cakes?

For custom multi-tier designs or special flavour pairings, we recommend booking 24–48 hours in advance so our head pastry chef can prepare your handcrafted cake.

Can I pick up my order directly from the bakehouse counter?

Yes, simply select pickup at checkout or give us a call. We will have your freshly boxed order ready with your name at our counter.
