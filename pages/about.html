OUR STORY & HERITAGE

Incorporating family recipes and heritage.

“Every batch begins with time-tested family recipes, patient hands, and ingredients we are genuinely proud to share.”

The essence of Cakery Charm lies in blending this rich baking heritage with contemporary pastry craft, creating a welcoming neighborhood space where each treat feels like a warm memory served with love in Phnom Penh.

CRAFTED DAILY

A few words about our bakehouse.

Each loaf on our counter is crafted from unbleached flour, pure butter, and natural starters. We believe good bread takes time to ferment and develop its signature golden crust and honeycomb crumb.

“Each dish on the table was made with devotion, reflecting the care and passion that went into preparing it for friends and family.”

OUR ROOTS

Neighborhood warm atmosphere.

Founded in 2015 by Emma Thompson, Cakery Charm began as a cozy corner bakehouse designed to make customers feel instantly at home.

Picture sunlit wooden counters, the gentle aroma of freshly roasted beans, and trays of warm brioche emerging from stone deck ovens every morning.
