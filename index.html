THE ARTISAN STANDARD

Baking without shortcuts.

Honest ingredients • Slow fermentation

🧈 01

100% French AOP Butter

We never use margarine, shortening, or artificial flavourings. Every croissant and brioche is folded with pure Normandy cream butter for incomparable aroma, crisp exterior, and melt-in-your-mouth honeycomb flake.

81 Flaky Layers Zero Trans Fats
🌾 02

72-Hour Wild Starter Fermentation

Our sourdough mothers rest in slow cold fermentation, naturally breaking down gluten proteins for gentle digestibility, deep caramelized crusts, and an authentic airy open crumb structure.

Prebiotic Starter No Added Sugar
🛵 03

Insulated Citywide Dispatch

Baked warm at dawn and carefully hand-packaged in compostable kraft bakery boxes with thermal seals. Delivered express across Phnom Penh or ready for pickup at our Boeung Tumpun counter.

30–45 Mins Delivery Eco-Friendly Kraft
